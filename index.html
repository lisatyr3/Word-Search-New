    How to play

    1. Find each word from the list hidden in the letter grid.
    2. Tap the first letter of a word, then tap its last letter. You can also tap each letter in order.
    3. Words run across, down, or on a slant, and a few run backwards.
    4. Stuck? Tap Hint to light up the first letter of a word.

    A new puzzle arrives every day at midnight Eastern time. Your progress is saved if you step away.
